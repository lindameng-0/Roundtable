What are you trying to revise?

Start with a question your draft can answer. These worked examples show how to use Readerfold's AI reader perspectives, passage-level reactions, and editorial report to make a specific revision decision.

Opening chapter feedback

An opening can withhold the answer and still give readers their bearings. Use Readerfold to examine whether your first chapter creates curiosity about the story you mean to tell, or leaves readers unsure what is happening.

Pacing feedback

Slow prose is not automatically a pacing problem. A pause can deepen anticipation, give a loss room to land, or interrupt a moment that needs to move. Readerfold helps you connect a reader's loss of momentum to the passage behind it.

Character motivation feedback

Readers can dislike a decision and still believe the character would make it. Use Readerfold to explore that distinction when a betrayal, sacrifice, or change of heart feels unearned in your draft.
