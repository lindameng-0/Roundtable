Where AI stops

Fast perspective. Human judgment.

Readerfold simulates reader reactions. It does not represent a real audience, predict sales, or replace the lived experience of human beta readers and editors. AI can miss context, overstate a concern, or share the same model bias across several perspectives.

Use it to explore a draft privately, identify passages worth another look, and prepare sharper questions for people who know your genre. You remain the author—and the final editor.

Before you bring your draft

What is Readerfold?

Readerfold is a panel of AI beta readers for fiction writers, designed to simulate how different people might experience a story. Up to five readers bring distinct tastes, voices, and perspectives. Follow their reactions beside the passages that prompted them, compare where they connect or disagree, and use the editorial report to consider your next revision.

How does an AI beta reader work?

An AI beta reader uses a language model to simulate a reader’s experience of a story. In Readerfold, each reader has generated tastes, reading habits, and a voice. You can choose a focus for each reader, such as character connection or plot logic, and compare their curiosity, expectations, confusion, and interpretations as they read.

What makes Readerfold’s reading experience distinctive?

Readerfold centers the individual reader’s reaction: what draws them in, how they interpret a character, and where they hesitate or change their expectations. Different AI readers respond to the same manuscript in their own voices, so you can compare perspectives before reviewing the combined editorial report. Their reactions are simulations, not feedback from real people or a prediction of audience response.

Can AI feedback replace human beta readers or an editor?

AI feedback can help you explore a draft before sharing it with people. It can also miss context, misunderstand your intent, or give conflicting advice. Human beta readers bring lived experience, and professional editors bring judgment that an AI panel cannot guarantee. You decide which suggestions serve your story.

What can I upload?

You can paste your writing or upload a TXT, DOCX, or PDF file. Confirm the extracted text, genre, and intended audience before starting a reading.

Can I try Readerfold for free?

You can create an account and try Readerfold with free starter credits without a payment card. Credit use varies with manuscript length, reader setup, panel size, and additional reports. The Pricing page explains the current allowances and paid options.

Will my manuscript be used to train AI?

Readerfold does not use your manuscript to train AI models, and we have opted out of training with our AI providers. We send relevant manuscript text to those providers to generate analysis and feedback. Training opt-outs do not mean zero retention; the Privacy policy explains provider processing and storage.

Will my manuscript be published or shared publicly?

No. Uploading to Readerfold does not publish your manuscript or make your feedback public. You keep your rights to your writing, and we do not acquire publishing rights or permission to use it in public marketing. Relevant content is processed by the service providers described in our Privacy policy.

Do you store my manuscript, and can I delete it?

Yes. Readerfold saves your manuscript and feedback in your account so you can return to them. You can delete a manuscript or contact support to request deletion. Active readings must finish before manuscript deletion. Deletion may not immediately remove backup copies, and external providers follow their own retention arrangements. See the Privacy policy for details and account deletion requests.
